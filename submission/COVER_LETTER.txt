Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript “Forecast-to-Trade under Information Constraints: Frozen Out-of-Sample Evidence from the Germany--Luxembourg Day-Ahead Electricity Market” for publication in Energy Economics.

The study asks whether improvements in day-ahead electricity-price forecasting survive point-in-time information constraints and translate into economically useful storage decisions. Models are developed on 2019--2025 public ENTSO-E data and evaluated on a first-look January--July 2026 holdout under a forecast-to-trade protocol whose model-selection, uncertainty, economic and sensitivity rules were specified and frozen before holdout exposure.

The Full XGBoost model reduces holdout MAE by about 40% relative to lag-24 and produces a positive S2--S1 economic contrast in all nine pre-specified efficiency--cost sensitivity cells. The paper also documents two qualifications that are economically important: the uncertainty-aware rule is algebraically a day-varying abstention threshold that exchanges aggregate profit for downside protection, and post-hoc robustness analysis shows that a simple trailing hourly profile captures most of the economic gain over lag-24 despite substantially worse point-forecast accuracy.

The paper’s contribution is therefore not an algorithmic novelty claim. It is a reproducible forecast-to-decision design that integrates information timing, chronological model selection, uncertainty, economic value, sensitivity and tail-risk diagnostics. This focus fits Energy Economics’ scope in energy-market forecasting, storage value, risk and economically meaningful model evaluation. The repository preserves the frozen protocol, model artifacts, holdout inputs, machine-readable outputs, supplementary post-hoc diagnostics and a replication guide mapping manuscript results to code and data.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all reported results have been checked against the preserved research artifacts.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
